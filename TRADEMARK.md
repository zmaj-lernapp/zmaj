replacing the Bosnian flag; the flag (`web/flagge.png`) is still used inside
the app and is not a mark of ours.

## You may, without asking

- say truthfully that your project is "based on Zmaj" or "uses the Zmaj
  vocabulary dataset", with a link to this repository;
- use the name in articles, talks, reviews, teaching material and research;
- run unmodified builds for yourself, your class or your organisation;
- keep the name in the code, the git history and the attribution notices.

## Please ask first

- publishing a modified app in an app store under the name "Zmaj" or with our
  icon. Rename it and replace the icon, so users can tell the two apart;
- using the marks for a product, domain, social media account or merchandise.

The dragon mascot (`web/maskottchen/`: the animation and its image parts) is
licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0 like the rest of the content, so you may reuse it
with attribution, but please do not present it as the official app's mascot
in a fork.

The mascot images and the app icon are AI-generated: illustrations created
with Google Gemini and edited by us (see `REUSE.toml` and `web/LIZENZEN.txt`).
Whether such images are protected by copyright differs by jurisdiction; the
CC BY-SA 4.0 license applies where rights exist. This trademark policy does
not depend on it: it is about using the icon to identify an app, not about
copying the picture.
